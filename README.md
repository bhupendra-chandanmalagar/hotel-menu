<h1>🏨 Hotel Menu Website</h1>

<h2>📌 About the Project</h2>
The Hotel Menu Website is a simple and user-friendly website developed using HTML5 and CSS3. It showcases a variety of food items and beverages available on a hotel menu.
<br><br> It allows users to explore different food items, categories, and prices in an organized way. 

<h2>✨ Project Highlights</h2>

- Organized food menu and categories.
- Clear display of food items and prices.
- Simple and user-friendly structure.

<h2>🛠️ Technologies Used</h2>

- <b>HTML5</b> - Structure and pages of the hotel menu.
- <b>CSS3</b> - Stylng, layout, and card design (internal CSS).
